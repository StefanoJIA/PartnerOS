What IntelliOpus connects

One bridge from product interest to delivery visibility.

The portal is built for approved supplier programs, not a single brand. Each supplier keeps its own catalog, resources, quote logic, order rules, and feedback path inside one customer workspace.

03 Execution

One operating bridge across suppliers.

Manufacturer side
