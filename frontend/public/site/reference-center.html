Future supplier resources

A separate resource lane for each new supplier program.

Future manufacturers can enter the same IntelliOpus portal without mixing their catalogs, manuals, sample rules, quote references, or delivery expectations with HOSUN or JOBO.

Program-specific files Each supplier keeps its own customer-safe catalogs, product resources, installation references, sample guidance, and approved delivery documents.
Customer-safe publishing Only approved public resources appear here. Internal cost, margin, supplier notes, private operations records, and unapproved files stay out of the customer portal.
