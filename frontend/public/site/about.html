Service history

Service history: from one program to a broader platform.

The first workflow came from real promotion, quoting, order intake, and delivery follow-up. That experience now becomes IntelliOpus: a supplier-neutral service layer for HOSUN, JOBO, and future qualified manufacturers.

Product promotion

HOSUN lifting systems provided the first structured path for catalog review, local samples, customer quoting, and order follow-up.

Quote discipline

Interval pricing, customer-safe PDF output, quote history, and internal cost boundaries became core operating requirements.

Supplier expansion

JOBO education furniture introduced a different project model: school procurement timing, classroom deployment, resource files, and delivery consistency.

Portal separation

The customer portal now separates public product and order visibility from internal supplier coordination, cost, margin, and operating records.

Current direction

IntelliOpus is being prepared as a local-server operating platform for supplier programs, customer resources, orders, logistics, feedback, and commercial memory.

Operating principles

Clear public pages. Controlled workspace data.

Public pages explain who we are and how the service works. The signed-in workspace carries catalog review, resources, sample checks, order visibility, and feedback.

01

Supplier-neutral entry

HOSUN, JOBO, and future programs enter as peer supplier programs, not as platform defaults.

02

Customer-safe workspace

Customers see approved product, resource, order, shipment, and feedback information only.

03

Project-ready service

The portal supports catalog review, sample checks, quote preparation, order follow-up, and delivery coordination.

04

Reusable learning

Feedback, quote experience, and delivery signals can later improve product and supplier decisions inside IntelliOpus.
